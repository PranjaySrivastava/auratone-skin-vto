## 📜 Scene-by-Scene Script & Recording Directives

### Scene 1: The Problem (0:00 – 0:25)
**🎥 Visual Direction:**
- Start with a quick zoom-in on the AuraTone headline: *"Stop Guessing. Dress in True Harmony."*
- Quickly cut to a quick montage or graphic highlighting e-commerce return statistics.

**🎙️ Voiceover (Speaker):**
> *"Over 65% of online fashion returns happen because an outfit looked incredible on the studio model, but washed out the customer the moment they tried it on at home.*  
> *Existing virtual try-on tools only answer one question: 'Does this fit my silhouette?' But they completely ignore the most critical factor in personal style:*  
> *'Does this fabric wavelength complement my skin's unique undertone?'"*

---

### Scene 2: Introducing AuraTone (0:25 – 0:45)
**🎥 Visual Direction:**
- Show the AuraTone UI on screen (full browser view, smooth mouse movements).
- Hover over the feature badges: *Perfect Corp YouCam API*, *Generative Apparel VTO*, *Skin Harmony Engine*.

**🎙️ Voiceover (Speaker):**
> *"Meet AuraTone: the world's first AI-powered Skin Harmony and Apparel Virtual Try-On Engine.*  
> *By fusing Perfect Corp's industry-leading YouCam Skin AI with Generative Apparel VTO and an Agentic AI Colorist, AuraTone transforms retail from a guessing game into a scientifically curated styling experience."*

---

### Scene 3: Biometric Capture & Color Diagnosis (0:45 – 1:15)
**🎥 Visual Direction:**
- Click on a persona (e.g., Marcus or Elena) or upload a portrait.
- Click **"Analyze Skin Harmony & Curate Wardrobe"**.
- Show the glowing holographic scanning ring and real-time biometric telemetry cycling on screen.
- Transition smoothly into the **Color Diagnosis** dashboard: show the detected Undertone (*Warm Golden / Cool Rosy*), Seasonal Archetype (*Deep Autumn / True Winter*), and the 5 Harmonious vs Clashing color swatches.

**🎙️ Voiceover (Speaker):**
> *"Here's how it works. With a single selfie, webcam capture, or diverse model selection, AuraTone dispatches the image directly to the YouCam Skin AI API.*  
> *In real-time, the engine diagnoses melanin depth, sub-pigment balance, and skin clarity—mapping the user into their calibrated Seasonal Color Palette.*  
> *Instead of generic recommendations, the user instantly sees the 5 precise color frequencies that make their skin radiate, alongside the clashing shades that cause visual fatigue."*

---

### Scene 4: Generative Apparel VTO & Curated Bundles (1:15 – 1:55)
**🎥 Visual Direction:**
- Step into the **VTO & Wardrobe** view.
- Show the high-resolution virtual try-on render generated on the user's portrait.
- **Crucial Action:** Drag the **Interactive Before / After Split Slider** back and forth across the model's portrait to showcase the transformation.
- Switch between occasion tabs: *Executive Power*, *Evening Gala*, *Minimalist Casual*.
- Highlight the **3-in-1 Complete Look** (Hero Garment + Harmonious Lip Shade + Metallic Jewelry Accent).

**🎙️ Voiceover (Speaker):**
> *"Next, our Generative Apparel VTO pipeline kicks in.*  
> *Watch as YouCam digitally tailors harmonious garments directly onto the user's portrait. With our interactive Before and After split-slider, shoppers and stylists can inspect the contrast and drape in photorealistic fidelity.*  
> *Even better, AuraTone solves cross-category retail by curating complete 3-in-1 harmony looks: uniting the hero garment with matching cosmetic pigments and metallic jewelry accents—all calibrated to the user's undertone."*

## 💡 Quick Recording & Production Tips

1. **Resolution & Scaling:** Record at **1080p (1920x1080)** or **2K**. Set browser zoom to **100%** or **90%** so the full dashboard fits comfortably.
2. **Smooth Cursor Movements:** Move your mouse with intent. Pause 1 second after clicking buttons so viewers can see the UI animations.
3. **Audio Quality:** Use a clean external microphone. Speak with energy and enthusiasm.
4. **Interactive Highlight:** Make sure you show the **Before/After slider drag**—it is one of the most visually impressive features judges look for!
