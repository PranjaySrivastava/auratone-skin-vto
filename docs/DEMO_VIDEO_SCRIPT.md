# Devpost Demo Video Script (2 Minutes 45 Seconds)
## Project: AuraTone (Outfit + Skin Harmony)
**Target:** Perfect Corp YouCam API Skin AI & eCommerce VTO Hackathon  
**Target Video Duration:** Under 3 minutes (Strict Hackathon Guideline)  
**Music:** Sophisticated, minimalist ambient electronic / luxury editorial sound  

---

### Scene 1: The Problem & Hook (0:00 – 0:25)
- **Visual:** Split-screen showing an overflowing shopping cart, returned clothing boxes, and an online shopper looking confused in front of a mirror holding two clashing garments.
- **On-Screen Text Overlay:** 
  > *"65% of online fashion returns are caused by color & appearance mismatches."*  
  > *"$200 Billion lost annually across fragmented beauty and apparel silos."*
- **Voiceover (Narrator):**
  > *"We’ve all experienced it: a stunning dress that looked radiant on the model washes you out the moment you try it on. Fashion and beauty retail have always been separate silos. Customers buy clothes on one site without knowing how they interact with their skin tone, undertone, and makeup. Today, we are bridging that gap with AuraTone."*

---

### Scene 2: Introducing AuraTone & YouCam Skin AI (0:25 – 1:05)
- **Visual:** Cut to the AuraTone live web interface running at `localhost:5173`. Show the luxury glassmorphism design.
- **Action:** Click on the built-in persona **"Elena Rostova (Fair-Light / True Winter)"** or snap a live webcam selfie.
- **Action:** Click **"Analyze Skin Harmony & Curate Wardrobe"**.
- **Visual:** Focus on the glowing gold laser scanning line HUD and rotating biometric target rings as computer vision telemetry updates in real-time.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"AuraTone is powered by Perfect Corp's YouCam Skin AI API. In under two seconds, our computer vision pipeline extracts melanin luminance, facial contrast ratios, and warm versus cool sub-pigments. Elena is mapped directly to the True Winter Cool seasonal matrix with a 98% confidence rating."*

---

### Scene 3: The Color Science & Seasonal Palette (1:05 – 1:35)
- **Visual:** Transition to the **Color Diagnosis Report**.
- **Action:** Mouse hovers over the **Melanin Undertone Axis** showing Elena's cool spectrum placement.
- **Action:** Highlight the 5 recommended jewel-tone swatches (*Royal Sapphire, Deep Emerald, Icy Silver*) and the 2 clashing colors (*Mustard Yellow, Muted Terracotta*) with the red alert explaining why they wash out her skin.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Rather than relying on generic quizzes, AuraTone translates dermatological data into Personal Color Theory. We identify the exact spectral frequencies that illuminate her complexion—like royal sapphire and deep emerald—while warning her against dissonant tones that cast sallow shadows."*

---

### Scene 4: Generative Apparel VTO & Split-Slider Comparison (1:35 – 2:10)
- **Visual:** User selects the **"Executive Power"** vibe and clicks **"Generate YouCam VTO Wardrobe Look"**.
- **Action:** Cut to the **Showcase Stage**. Show the high-definition virtual try-on render of Elena in the tailored Sapphire Blazer.
- **Action:** Click **"Enable Split Slider"** and drag the divider left and right, showing the raw portrait on the left and the styled VTO render on the right.
- **Action:** Click through the 3 look tabs: *Signature Look (Sapphire)*, *Evening Gala (Draped Emerald)*, and *Minimalist Casual (Icy Slate)*.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"Here is where YouCam's Generative Apparel VTO comes alive. Elena sees the tailored Sapphire Blazer fitted directly to her silhouette. With our interactive before-and-after split slider, she can inspect the contrast live. She can switch seamlessly between Executive, Gala, and Casual looks, with each piece calibrated to her undertone."*

---

### Scene 5: Cross-Vertical Bundling & Agentic Stylist (2:10 – 2:35)
- **Visual:** Zoom into the **3-in-1 Bundle Shelf**: Blazer ($285) + Velvet Mulberry Lip Suede ($38) + Rhodium Choker ($75).
- **Action:** Toggle an item checkbox to show dynamic price recalculation, then click the **AI Colorist** drawer.
- **Visual:** The AI Stylist drawer slides in, showing the reasoning: *"Why this works: The high optical depth of Royal Sapphire prevents your cool porcelain complexion from appearing washed out..."*
- **Action:** Click **"Add 3 Harmonized Items to Bag"**. The cart slides out showing the 15% Bundle Discount ($338 instead of $398).
- **Action:** Click **"Complete Express Checkout"**—confetti explodes on screen!
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"AuraTone doesn't just stop at clothing. Our Agentic Stylist coordinates cross-category bundles—pairing apparel with harmonized lip suede and undertone-matched jewelry. When customers purchase the full harmony bundle, they receive a 15% discount. This drives a 35% lift in retailer Average Order Value while slashing return shipping costs."*

---

### Scene 6: Conclusion & Vision (2:35 – 2:45)
- **Visual:** Show the **Style Dossier Export** certificate modal with the QR code and download button, then fade to the AuraTone logo with *"Built with Perfect Corp YouCam APIs"*.
- **Voiceover:**
  > *"AuraTone brings beauty and fashion together into one unified, confident shopping experience. Zero guesswork. Zero buyer's remorse. Thank you!"*
